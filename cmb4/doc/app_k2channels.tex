\section{Machine-generated channel kernels of \texorpdfstring{$K_2$}{K2}}
\label{app:k2channels}
This appendix reproduces the channel kernels of $K_2(\vk_1,\vk_2;\nhat)$ exactly as generated by
\code{cmb4/code/A2\_second\_order/symbolic.py} (output \code{symbolic\_kernels.pkl}, \code{symbolic\_kernels.tex}) from the
direct-form term list of $L_2/2$ (\code{L\_terms.pkl}, Sec.~\ref{sec:map2}), with the symbol names of
Sec.~\ref{sec:asm-K2} substituted typographically (no algebra was changed; the script that performs the substitution
also verifies symbolically that every expression below equals the compact form quoted in
Sec.~\ref{sec:asm-channels}: residual $0$ for all fifteen channels).  Each expression is already the contribution to $K_2$,
i.e.\ it includes the factor 2 of $K_2=2\,{\rm Sym}[K_{L_2/2}]+\dots$ and the symmetrisation $1\leftrightarrow2$.
Notation (Sec.~\ref{sec:asm-K2}): $\mu=\khat_1\cdot\khat_2$, $\mu_a=\nhat\cdot\khat_a$, $\vK=\vk_1+\vk_2$, $K_n=\nhat\cdot\vK=k_1\mu_1+k_2\mu_2$,
$P_\perp=k_1k_2(\mu-\mu_1\mu_2)$, $E_a=e^{ik_a\mu_a\chi}$, $E_K=E_1E_2$; first-order transfer functions $T_{X,a}\equiv T_X(k_a,\eta)$ and
their $\eta$-derivatives $\dot T_{X,a}$; ray functionals $\mathcal F_a$, $\mathcal D_{\perp,a}$, $\mathcal D_{r,a}$ \eqref{eq:asm-functionals};
observer and emission objects $X_{o,a}$, $\delta\eta_{o,a}$, $\delta\eta_{e,a}$ \eqref{eq:asm-clock}--\eqref{eq:asm-emission};
second-order pair kernels $\Phi_2,\dot\Phi_2,\Psi_2,\dot\Psi_2,\delta_{T2},V_2$ and $K^{(ab)}_{\omega}\equiv K_\omega(\vk_a,\vk_b)$,
$K^{(ab)}_{v^T}$, $\mathcal H$, $\dot{\mathcal H}$ \eqref{eq:asm-second}.  The numerical value of every channel agrees with the
independent numerical engine (\code{kernel2.py}) to $\le1.8\times10^{-15}$ (\code{check\_symbolic.log}).
The type-B products are $K_1(\vk_1)K_1(\vk_2)-\Delta_0(k_1)K_1(\vk_2)-\Delta_0(k_2)K_1(\vk_1)$ \eqref{eq:asm-K2}.
