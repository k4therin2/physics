\begin{abstract}
We set up the calculation of the observed temperature angular power spectrum
$C_\ell=\eps^2C_\ell^{(11)}+\eps^4[\tfrac14C_\ell^{(22)}+\tfrac13\Re C_\ell^{(13)}]+O(\eps^6)$
in a spatially flat, radiation-only universe governed by general relativity with $\Lambda=0$,
for photons emitted on the physical hypersurface $T_{\rm phys}=T_*$ and observed by a tagged
fluid element at fixed proper time.  The settled parts are presented here: (i)~an exact,
non-perturbative reformulation of the measurement, $T_{\rm obs}(\nhat)=T_o\,e^{-S(\nhat)}$ with
$S$ the fluid shear integrated along the ray, which contains all Sachs--Wolfe, Doppler,
integrated Sachs--Wolfe, lensing, time-delay, emission-surface and observer physics;
(ii)~the definition of the Gaussian non-linear primordial variable and the construction of
regular initial data satisfying the constraints at every order; (iii)~the perturbative solution
of the Einstein--fluid system through third order in Poisson gauge (closed first-order transfer
functions, second-order scalar/vector/tensor kernels and Green's functions, third-order
contracted kernels); (iv)~the exact normalisation bookkeeping and the harmonic-space machinery
needed to turn direction-space kernels into $C_\ell^{(22)}$ and $C_\ell^{(13)}$.  Sections that
assemble the explicit second- and third-order maps, the final $C_\ell$, the checks and the
monochromatic reduction are marked as placeholders to be supplied.  Every result quoted
here has been verified symbolically or numerically; the verification ledger lists each check.
\end{abstract}
