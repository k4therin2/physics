\begin{abstract}
We compute the observed temperature angular power spectrum
$C_\ell=\eps^2C_\ell^{(11)}+\eps^4[\tfrac14C_\ell^{(22)}+\tfrac13\Re C_\ell^{(13)}]+O(\eps^6)$
of a spatially flat, radiation-only universe in general relativity ($\Lambda=0$, perfect fluid $\rho=\alpha\Tphys^4$), for a
Gaussian non-linear primordial curvature perturbation $\zeta_{\rm nl}=\eps\zeta_1$, photons emitted isotropically in the fluid
rest frame on the hypersurface $\Tphys=T_*$, and an observer comoving with the fluid who observes at fixed proper time after
the crossing of $\Tphys=T_i$.  Three structural results organise the calculation.  (i)~The measurement is exactly
$\Tobs(\nhat)=T_o\,e^{-S(\nhat)}$, with $S$ the fluid shear integrated along the ray; it contains all Sachs--Wolfe, Doppler,
integrated Sachs--Wolfe, lensing, time-delay, emission-surface and observer effects, and the normalised anisotropy is
$1+\Theta=e^{-S}/\sky{e^{-S}}$.  (ii)~Gaussianity of $\zeta_{\rm nl}$, regularity at $\eta=0$ and the absence of primordial vector and
tensor modes fix the initial data at every order; they are non-zero, non-local and satisfy the constraints.  (iii)~The
normalisation and the order-by-order maps $\Theta^{(n)}$ follow by exact algebra from $\Lambda=-S$.  We give the first-order
transfer function and $C^{(11)}_\ell$ in closed form; all second-order fields in closed form (elementary functions, $\Si$ and $\Ci$);
the contracted third-order fields with closed-form initial amplitude and one declared time integral; the complete
line-of-sight maps $L_1,L_2,L_3$ (all terms are listed); the harmonic machinery; the kernel $K_2$, $C^{(22)}_\ell$ and its
monochromatic evaluation; the contracted third-order kernel, $C^{(13)}_\ell$ and the monochromatic $O(\eps^4)$ coefficient (an evaluation by
quadrature at one parameter point).  Checks: the exact formula, the
field equations, the second-order closed forms and the harmonic rules are verified symbolically or to high precision;
the maps $\Theta^{(1,2,3)}$ are verified end to end against a non-perturbative solver in a different gauge; the contracted
third-order kernel is checked by form equivalence, residual-gauge invariance and its squeezed limit; the cancellation of the collinear
poles of the $(13)$ integrand and the UV behaviour of the loop are established numerically only, and the formulas (E2), (E3),
$T^{\rm(ii)}$ and an analytic zero-mode proof are open (Sec.~\ref{sec:unresolved}).
\end{abstract}
