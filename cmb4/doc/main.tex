\begin{abstract}
We compute the observed temperature angular power spectrum
$C_\ell=\eps^2C_\ell^{(11)}+\eps^4[\tfrac14C_\ell^{(22)}+\tfrac13\Re C_\ell^{(13)}]+O(\eps^6)$
of a spatially flat, radiation-only universe in general relativity ($\Lambda=0$, perfect fluid $\rho=\alpha\Tphys^4$), for a
Gaussian non-linear primordial curvature perturbation $\zeta_{\rm nl}=\eps\zeta_1$, photons emitted isotropically in the fluid
rest frame on the hypersurface $\Tphys=T_*$, and an observer comoving with the fluid who observes at fixed proper time after
the crossing of $\Tphys=T_i$.  Three structural results organise the calculation.  (i)~The measurement is exactly
$\Tobs(\nhat)=T_o\,e^{-S(\nhat)}$, with $S$ the fluid shear integrated along the ray; it contains all Sachs--Wolfe, Doppler,
integrated Sachs--Wolfe, lensing, time-delay, emission-surface and observer effects, and $1+\Theta=e^{-S}/\sky{e^{-S}}$.
(ii)~Gaussianity of $\zeta_{\rm nl}$, regularity at $\eta=0$ and the absence of primordial vector and tensor modes fix the initial data at
every order; they are non-zero, non-local and satisfy the constraints.  (iii)~The normalisation and the maps $\Theta^{(n)}$ follow by exact
algebra from $\Lambda=-S$.  We give the first-order transfer function and $C^{(11)}_\ell$ in closed form; all second-order fields in closed
form (elementary functions, $\Si$ and $\mathrm{Cin}$); the contracted third-order fields with closed-form sources and initial amplitude and one
time integral; the complete line-of-sight maps $L_1,L_2,L_3$; the loop formulas for $C^{(22)}_\ell$ and $C^{(13)}_\ell$ in named kernels; the
squeezed limits of $K_2$ and $K_3$, which make the infrared region of the loop finite for $n_s\ge1$ and equal to $\tfrac12(n_s-1)^2\sigma_L^2C^{(11)}_\ell$
for a power law; and the monochromatic result as a boxed expression in these objects, evaluated by quadrature at one parameter point
($k_0\eta_*=2$, $k_0\eta_o=8$; $C^{(22)}_\ell$ also at $k_0\eta_o=4$).  We prove that the collinear poles of the $(13)$ integrand are a residual
time-reparametrisation mode of Poisson gauge whose residues cancel identically, and that the only residual zero-mode freedom is this time
reparametrisation, under which the map is invariant.  Checks: the exact formula, the field equations, the closed forms and the harmonic rules
symbolically or to high precision; the maps $\Theta^{(1,2,3)}$ end to end against a non-perturbative solver in a different gauge; $C^{(22)}_\ell$ by
an independent Monte Carlo; the contracted third-order kernel by form equivalence, residual-gauge invariance and its squeezed limit.  Open: the
ultraviolet behaviour of the loop for power-law spectra, probed numerically only up to $q=4k$, and a numerical test of the harmonic formula $T^{\rm(ii)}$,
which the evaluation does not use (Sec.~\ref{sec:unresolved}).
\end{abstract}
