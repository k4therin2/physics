I threw my Claude at your CMB problem

An ~8-hour multi-agent run (spread over two days, paused by a usage limit) on “Analytical temperature power spectrum through fourth order” (radiation-only GR universe). This page is the summary; everything behind it is in the repo.

Where to look: 2-page summary (SUMMARY.md) Manuscript PDF (109 pp, roadmap in the first 10) Agent notes & referee reports Code (sympy engines, solver, checks) Branch cmb-fourth-order

Two-sentence takeaway

For a pure radiation fluid the observed temperature is exactly the local temperature times exp of minus the fluid shear integrated along the ray, which collapses the Sachs–Wolfe / Doppler / lensing / emission-surface bookkeeping into one integral and makes the third-order expansion tractable. The full fourth-order spectrum (C11, C22 and the previously uncomputed 1×3 term) is assembled with all observer and normalisation terms, evaluated for a monochromatic spectrum, and checked end-to-end against an exact numerical GR solution in a different gauge at first, second and third order.

How much of your document got addressed

Done: Gaussian variable and “no higher-order primordial modes” defined precisely; initial data satisfying the constraints at every order; emission on T = T*, fluid-worldline observer at fixed proper time from a physical initial event, Fermi–Walker tetrad (shown to drop out); normalise-then-remove-dipole in the stated order with all observer and endpoint terms; Θ through third order with every induced vector/tensor term (the 359-term third-order functional is printed in full); every Fourier, convolution and angular normalisation fixed; finite and distinct ηi, η*, ηo; monochromatic specialisation with the variance held fixed; explicit list of unresolved steps. All analytic; numerics appear only as checks and for one illustrative table.

Not done / partial: third-order time integrals left as explicit one-dimensional integrals (second order is fully closed-form); the 1×3 term evaluated at one parameter point and at ηo/η* = 4 rather than a CMB-like 50; ultraviolet behaviour for power-law spectra only probed numerically (no damping in a perfect fluid, so a log divergence is expected); no test under a general second-order gauge transformation, only the k = 0 family plus the different-gauge numerical comparison.

Is any of it new?

  • The shear formula: a special case of an exact line-of-sight result in Dunsby 1997 (CQG 14, 3391), who wrote it for general matter and then linearised. The explicit exponential-shear form and its corollary (shear-free radiation flow ⇒ isotropic sky for every comoving observer) were not found stated anywhere; the manuscript credits Dunsby.
  • The fourth-order spectrum: two literature searches found no complete one-loop CMB temperature spectrum including the 1×3 term (Tomita 2007 explicitly left it out; SONG’s scope excludes it). Appears new, but in a toy radiation-only model.
  • Reusable tools: a sympy Einstein-equation engine to third order, closed forms for every second-order field in radiation domination, and a high-precision planar-symmetric GR + radiation solver with ray tracing.
