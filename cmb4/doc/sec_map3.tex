\section{The third-order map \texorpdfstring{$\Theta^{(3)}$}{Theta3}}
\label{sec:map3}
The third-order map is built with exactly the machinery of Sec.~\ref{sec:map2}: the integrand $\mathcal I$ of \eqref{eq:Idef}
expanded to $O(\eps^3)$ with $k^i=-n^i+\eps k_1^i+\tfrac{\eps^2}2k_2^i$ (the $k_3$ and $\Delta x_3$ terms do not enter, since
$\mathcal I=O(\eps)$), the position expansion to second order in $\Delta\vx=\eps\Delta\vx_1+\tfrac{\eps^2}2\Delta\vx_2$, the endpoint formula
\eqref{eq:endpoint} with $\delta\eta_o=\eps\delta\eta_{o1}+\tfrac{\eps^2}2\delta\eta_{o2}$ and $\delta\eta_e=\eps\delta\eta_{e1}+\tfrac{\eps^2}2\delta\eta_{e2}$,
and the functionals \eqref{eq:k1def}--\eqref{eq:Dx2def}, \eqref{eq:deta_o1}--\eqref{eq:deta_e}.  The new ingredients relative to $L_2$
are the second-order functionals $k_2$, $\Delta\vx_2$, $\delta\eta_{o2}$, $\delta\eta_{e2}$, the triad and direction map at second order
\eqref{eq:triad}, \eqref{eq:kinit2}, the second-order fields (including $\omega_2$, $\Phi_2$, $\Psi_2$, which now appear in products), and the
third-order fields in the local shear.

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$L_3$}{L3}: named groups}
\label{sec:L3}
The expansion gives $\tfrac16L_3$ as 359 terms in 32 groups for $\nhat=\hat{\mathbf z}$.  Table~\ref{tab:L3groups} organises the groups by
the three endpoints and by physical content; \emph{every term of every group is displayed in App.~\ref{app:L3full}}, so that $\Theta^{(3)}$ is
fully explicit: $L_3$ from App.~\ref{app:L3full}, $L_1,L_2$ from App.~\ref{app:lterms}, and \eqref{eq:theta3} below.
The structure is that of $L_2$ promoted by one order: every second-order piece (a)--(g) of Table~\ref{tab:L2pieces} reappears
(1)~with one first-order field replaced by a second-order field, (2)~multiplied by one more first-order factor (a field, a
functional $k_1$, $\Delta\vx_1$, or a shift $\delta\eta_{o1},\delta\eta_{e1}$), or (3)~with a first-order functional replaced by its
second-order counterpart ($k_2$, $\Delta\vx_2$, $\delta\eta_{o2}$, $\delta\eta_{e2}$).  The one genuinely new piece is the third-order shear,
\begin{equation}
\tfrac16L_3\supset-\tfrac16\int_{\eta_*}^{\bar\eta_o}\Big(I_1[\vv_3]+\tfrac12n^in^jh'_{3,ij}\Big)\dd\eta
\qquad(\text{5 terms for }\nhat=\hat{\mathbf z}),
\label{eq:L3loc3}
\end{equation}
and, as at second order, $\omega_3,\Phi_3,\Psi_3$ do not appear in the shear form (they do in the direct form).
The second-order functionals carry the second-order physics of the ray: $k_2$ contains the second-order aberration
\eqref{eq:kinit2}, the transport of the initial direction to the background event, and the second-order deflection
$\int[\tfrac12G_2+\Delta\vx_1\cdot\nabla G_1]$ (lens--lens coupling and the deflection by second-order potentials, $\omega_2$, $h_2$);
$\Delta\vx_2$ is the second-order displacement (post-Born lensing and time delay); $\delta\eta_{e2}$ the emission surface at second
order including the lensed position of $\delta_{T,1}$ \eqref{eq:deta_e}; $\delta\eta_{o2}$ the second-order clock \eqref{eq:deta_o2}.

\subsection{From \texorpdfstring{$L_3$}{L3} to \texorpdfstring{$\Theta^{(3)}$}{Theta3}}
\label{sec:theta3}
By \eqref{eq:mdef} and \eqref{eq:theta_orders},
\begin{equation}
m_3=L_3+3L_1L_2+L_1^3,\qquad
\Theta^{(3)}_{\ell m}=m_{3,\ell m}-3\sky{m_1}m_{2,\ell m}-3\sky{m_2}m_{1,\ell m}+6\sky{m_1}^2m_{1,\ell m}\quad(\ell\ge1),
\label{eq:theta3}
\end{equation}
obtained from the exponential and ratio series (App.~\ref{app:ledger}, R-5).  Only the correlation of
$\Theta^{(3)}$ with $\Theta^{(1)}$ is needed, $C^{(13)}_\ell$ of \eqref{eq:loops}, decomposed as in (E0) of Sec.~\ref{sec:thirteen}.

\paragraph{What survives the self-contraction.}  In $\langle m_{1}\,m_3^*\rangle$ one $\zeta$ of $m_3$ is paired with $m_1$ and the
remaining two are contracted with each other.  Qualitatively the groups of Table~\ref{tab:L3groups} and the products $3L_1L_2$,
$L_1^3$ fall into four classes (their explicit evaluation is the subject of Sec.~\ref{sec:assembly}):
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \emph{Variance $\times$ map.}  Products of three first-order objects (cubic local terms; $k_1^2$, $(\Delta\vx_1)^2$, $k_1\Delta\vx_1$,
$\delta\eta_{o1}^2$, $\delta\eta_{e1}^2$, \ldots; first-order parts of $L_1L_2$ and $L_1^3$): contracting two of the three factors leaves a
one-point variance or an equal-sky-point correlation times a first-order map, structure (i) of Sec.~\ref{sec:thirteen}.  The
$(\Delta\vx_{1\perp})^2\partial\partial I_1$ group, for instance, produces the convergence-type term $-\ell(\ell+1)R\,C_\ell$ of the lensing
control case (Sec.~\ref{sec:lensing_control}), now with the radiation-era deflection correlated with the source.
\item[(ii)] \emph{Tadpole (mean field).}  A second-order field $X_2$ times a first-order factor, with the first-order factor paired with
$m_1$: the contraction rule $A_1B_2\to A_k\langle B_2\rangle$ of \eqref{eq:contraction_rules} gives the mean field $\langle X_2\rangle(\eta)$ (a
function of time only) times a first-order map.  Vector, tensor and velocity tadpoles vanish by isotropy, so only $\langle\Phi_2\rangle$,
$\langle\Psi_2\rangle$, $\langle\delta_{T,2}\rangle$ survive, and the groups that contain them are: the local (2+1) group ($\Phi_2,\Psi_2$ undifferentiated);
the second-order clock $\delta\eta_{o2}$ ($\Psi_2$ on the worldline, \eqref{eq:deta_o2}); the second-order emission shift $\delta\eta_{e2}$ ($\delta_{T,2}$,
\eqref{eq:deta_e}); and the radial direction functionals $k^z_2$, $\Delta x^z_2$ (through $k^z_{2,\rm init}\supset-\tfrac12(\Psi_2+\Phi_2)$, \eqref{eq:kinit2},
and $\partial_\eta\langle\Phi_2+\Psi_2\rangle$ in $G^z_2$).  Hence
\begin{equation}
\Theta^{(3)}_{c,\rm tad}(\vk;\nhat)=\sum_{X\in\{\Phi_2,\Psi_2,\delta_{T,2}\}}\mathcal T_X\big[\langle X_2\rangle\big](\vk;\nhat),
\label{eq:tadgroup}
\end{equation}
where $\mathcal T_X$ is the linear map obtained by replacing $X_2(\eta,\vx)\to\langle X_2\rangle(\eta)$ in the listed groups of App.~\ref{app:L3full}
(and in $3L_1L_2$), keeping the first-order factor as a Fourier mode $\vk$.  Individual tadpoles shift under the residual time reparametrisation of
Sec.~\ref{sec:asm-zeromodes}; only their sum with the clock, emission-surface and normalisation terms is invariant.
\item[(iii)] \emph{Cross-contracted.}  The same products with the first-order factor contracted with one leg of $X_2$ (rule
$A_1B_2\to2A_qK_B(-\vq,\vk)$), and the cross-pairings of three first-order factors at different points: a loop convolution, structure (ii)
of Sec.~\ref{sec:thirteen}.
\item[(iv)] \emph{Third-order fields.}  The local shear \eqref{eq:L3loc3} (and $\Phi_3,\Psi_3,\delta_{T,3}$ in the direct form), which enter only
through the contracted fields $X_{3c}$ of \eqref{eq:contracted}, including the amplitude $C_3$ \eqref{eq:C3}: structure (iii).
\end{enumerate}
The normalisation terms $-3\sky{m_1}m_2-3\sky{m_2}m_1+6\sky{m_1}^2m_1$ of \eqref{eq:theta3} supply the remaining pieces of (E0).

\paragraph{A worked contraction: the emission-shift tadpole.}  To show what a $(13)$ contribution looks like, take the group
``emission shift $\delta\eta_{e2}$'' (3 terms, App.~\ref{app:L3full}): by the endpoint formula \eqref{eq:endpoint}, $\tfrac16L_3\supset\tfrac12\delta\eta_{e2}\,I_1|_*$,
and by \eqref{eq:deta_e} $\delta\eta_{e2}\supset\eta_*\delta_{T,2}|_*$, so $L_3\supset3\eta_*\,\delta_{T,2}\,I_1|_*$.  For one Fourier mode,
$v_{1,i}=ik_iT_V\zeta_{\vk}$ gives $I_1=n^in^j\partial_iv_{1,j}-\tfrac13\partial\cdot v_1=-\tfrac23k^2T_V(k,\eta)P_2(\nhat\cdot\khat)\,e^{i\vk\cdot\nhat\chi}\zeta_{\vk}$.  The
contraction $A_1B_2\to A_k\langle B_2\rangle$ then contributes to the contracted map \eqref{eq:Theta3c}
\begin{gather}
\Theta^{(3)}_c(\vk;\nhat)\supset-2\eta_*\,\langle\delta_{T,2}\rangle(\eta_*)\,k^2T_V(k,\eta_*)\,P_2(\nhat\cdot\khat)\,e^{i\vk\cdot\nhat\chi_*},\nonumber\\
\langle\delta_{T,2}\rangle(\eta)=\int\!\frac{\dd^3q}{(2\pi)^3}P(q)\Big[\tfrac14\mathrm{tad}_{\delta_2}(q,\eta)-\tfrac3{16}T_\delta(q\eta/\sqrt3)^2\Big],
\end{gather}
using $\delta_{T,2}=\tfrac14\delta_2-\tfrac3{16}\delta_1^2$ and $\mathrm{tad}_{\delta_2}$ of App.~\ref{app:kernels},
i.e.\ $\mathsf h_2(k,\eta)\supset-2\eta_*\langle\delta_{T,2}\rangle k^2T_V\,\delta(\eta-\eta_*)$ in \eqref{eq:hL}, and by (A-$L$)
$\Delta^{(3c)}_\ell\supset-2\eta_*\langle\delta_{T,2}\rangle(\eta_*)k^2T_V(k,\eta_*)\sum_{J=\ell,\ell\pm2}(2J+1)(\begin{smallmatrix}\ell&2&J\\0&0&0\end{smallmatrix})^2i^{J-\ell}j_J(k\chi_*)$.
Physically this is the first-order emission-time Doppler/shear term with the emission surface displaced by the mean temperature
perturbation; it is matched by the $\langle\Psi_2\rangle$ in $\delta\eta_{o2}$ and by $-3\sky{m_2}m_1$, as the zero-mode argument requires.
The cross-contracted part of the same group, $2A_qK_{\delta_{T,2}}(-\vq,\vk)$, is a structure-(ii) loop term.  The
\emph{obs} groups are polynomials in $\nhat$ of bounded degree (all fields at the observer event, direction-independent shifts), so their
direct contribution to $C^{(13)}_\ell$ is confined to a few low multipoles; through the products $3L_1L_2$, $L_1^3$ and the normalisation the
observer quantities nevertheless affect all $\ell$.
