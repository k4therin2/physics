                                                           earities at recombination on all scales. We use CosmoLib2nd , a numerical Boltzmann code at
                                                           second-order to compute CMB bispectra on the full sky. We consistently include all effects except
                                                           gravitational lensing, which can be added to our result using standard methods. The bispectrum
                                                           is peaked on squeezed triangles and agrees with the analytic approximation in the squeezed limit
                                                           at the few per cent level for all the scales where this is applicable. On smaller scales, we recover

                                                                         local contamination may be biased by numerical errors.
                                                                         Our calculation did not include lensing and other late-
